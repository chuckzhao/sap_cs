# Level 1: Beginner - SAP CS Fundamentals

## Welcome to SAP Customer Service! 👋

Congratulations on starting your journey to becoming an SAP CS expert! This beginner module will build your foundation in SAP Customer Service.

## What You'll Learn in Level 1

1. **SAP CS Overview** - Understanding the big picture
2. **SAP Navigation** - Getting comfortable with the system
3. **Master Data** - The building blocks of SAP CS
4. **Basic Transactions** - Your first hands-on experience
5. **Organizational Structures** - How SAP CS is organized

## Time Commitment

- **Duration**: 4 weeks
- **Study Time**: 10-15 hours per week
- **Hands-on Practice**: 5-10 hours per week

## Completion Checklist

By the end of Level 1, you should be able to:

- [ ] Explain what SAP CS is and its business purpose
- [ ] Navigate confidently through SAP GUI
- [ ] Understand the key master data objects
- [ ] Create and display service notifications
- [ ] Create basic service orders
- [ ] Understand organizational structures
- [ ] Run basic standard reports
- [ ] Explain how SAP CS fits in the broader SAP ecosystem
